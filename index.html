DISCIPLINE & HYGIENE

Gym Rules & Etiquette

To ensure safety, supreme cleanliness, and fair equipment access for all members, everyone must adhere strictly to RFH rules:

No Outside Footwear

Strictly bring clean, dedicated indoor workout shoes.

Fees Non-Refundable & Non-Transferable

Membership fees once paid cannot be refunded or assigned to another person.

Return Dumbbells & Weights to Rack

Rerack all plates and dumbbells after your set. Keep the floor clean.

Maximum Treadmill Time: 15 Minutes

During peak hours to allow all athletes access to cardiovascular tech.
